Why This Matters for BTech Students

Business Strategy Is a Technical Skill

Engineers are trained to solve defined problems. But the most impactful engineers — the ones who build companies, lead products, or drive strategy — understand the business system around the code they write.

Studying Reliance isn't just studying business. It's studying systems thinking, infrastructure design, network effects, data moats, and capital allocation — all frameworks directly applicable to building technology products.

🧠

Systems Thinking

How does one decision ripple through an entire ecosystem? Reliance is a masterclass in thinking across multiple levels simultaneously.

📊

Business Model Analysis

Revenue streams, moats, competitive positioning, unit economics — these are as important as architecture decisions in software.

⚡

Product Strategy

Jio's launch strategy is one of the most instructive product launches in history. Pricing, timing, distribution — all of it.

🌐

Network Effects

Understanding how platforms grow and why incumbents fail to compete with new entrants — essential for any tech product builder.

💰

Capital Efficiency

How companies raise, allocate and deploy capital is the difference between companies that survive and those that dominate.

🔮

Long-Term Thinking

Building with a 10-year horizon instead of a 10-week sprint changes every decision — from architecture to team structure.
