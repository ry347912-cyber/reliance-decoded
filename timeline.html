Era I — The Founding
1966
Founding

Reliance Commercial Corporation Founded

Dhirubhai Ambani starts a yarn and spices trading business in Mumbai. Registered capital: ₹15,000. The ambition: limitless.

1975
Founding

Naroda Textile Plant — First Factory

Reliance sets up its first manufacturing facility at Naroda, Gujarat. The plant is sized for 3x industry-standard scale — a deliberate choice to dominate, not compete.

1977
Founding

Reliance Goes Public — Democratizing Investment

Reliance's IPO is oversubscribed 7x. Dhirubhai Ambani runs mass campaigns to bring retail investors into equity ownership — a first for Indian business. Over 58,000 shareholders from small towns invest.

Era II — Industrial Scale
1986
Growth

Petrochemicals — Moving Up the Value Chain

Reliance enters petrochemicals, beginning the vertical integration that would define its next 30 years. Instead of buying raw materials from others, Reliance builds the capacity to produce them.

1991
Growth

India Liberalizes — Reliance Is Ready

The 1991 economic liberalization opens India to competition and capital. Reliance, already scaled for global competitiveness, becomes the biggest private sector beneficiary of the new India.

1999
Growth

Jamnagar Refinery — World's Largest

The Jamnagar refinery begins operations. It becomes the world's largest refining complex, processing 1.24 million barrels per day. The scale was audacious — no private company had built anything like it.

2002
Transition

Dhirubhai Ambani Passes Away

The founder dies, leaving behind a $6 billion empire. The subsequent succession dispute between his sons Mukesh and Anil splits the group in 2005. Mukesh retains the core industries and capital-intensive businesses.

Era III — The Digital Revolution
2006
Digital

Reliance Retail Launches

India's organised retail story begins. Reliance Fresh, Reliance Digital, Reliance Trends — a multi-format retail empire is built. Today it is India's largest retailer with ₹2.7 lakh crore in revenue.

2010
Digital

4G Spectrum Acquired — Jio Begins

Reliance acquires pan-India 4G spectrum for ₹12,847 crore. For 6 years, it builds quietly — 1.2 million km of fiber, 200,000 towers — while the industry assumes they will never launch.

2016
Digital

Jio Launches — India's Data Revolution

September 2016. Free calls, 1GB/day free data. The telecom industry is upended overnight. Vodafone, Idea, Airtel lose millions of subscribers. 100 million users in 6 months. Data costs fall 95%. A billion people come online.

2020
Digital

₹1.52 Lakh Crore Raised in Six Months

Facebook (₹43,574 Cr), Google (₹33,737 Cr), KKR, Silver Lake, Mubadala and 10+ global investors buy stakes in Jio Platforms. Reliance becomes debt-free at the height of a global pandemic. The fundraising signals global confidence at exactly the right moment.

Era IV — The Green Future
2021
Future

New Energy — ₹75,000 Crore Bet on Green Hydrogen

Mukesh Ambani announces Reliance's pivot to New Energy. Solar panels, green hydrogen, batteries, electrolyzers — four giga-factories in Jamnagar. The bet: India's energy future is renewable, and Reliance will own the infrastructure.

2023
Future

JioCinema — Disrupting Media

JioCinema streams the IPL for free — the world's most watched cricket tournament. 32 million concurrent streams. OTT streaming costs drop. India's media industry transforms. Again.

2025
Future

Succession — Isha, Akash & Anant Take Charge

The third generation of Ambanis steps into leadership. Isha leads Retail, Akash leads Jio, Anant leads New Energy. The deliberate, gradual succession ensures continuity across India's most complex business empire.
